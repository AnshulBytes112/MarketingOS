# AI Brand Growth Engine - Product Requirements

## Core Concept
The AI Brand Growth Engine is an AI-native marketing operating system. It replaces the traditional linear marketing workflow with a continuous, self-optimizing loop:
**Observe → Analyze → Strategize → Plan → Create → Approve → Publish → Measure → Learn → Optimize → Repeat**

## 10 Functional Engines
1. **Brand Intelligence Engine:** Ingests brand assets and builds the "Brand DNA" (voice, tone, positioning).
2. **Competitor Intelligence Engine:** Tracks competitor content, posting cadence, and identifies gaps.
3. **Market Intelligence Engine:** Social listening, trend detection, and topic opportunity surfacing.
4. **Strategy Engine:** Converts research into content pillars, funnel mapping, and monthly strategy.
5. **Content Engine:** Generates platform-specific text, image, and video content (source-based or AI-native).
6. **Campaign Engine:** Groups content into managed campaigns with budgets and timelines.
7. **Publishing Engine:** Manages OAuth, scheduling, and publishing across platforms.
8. **Analytics Engine:** Collects and normalizes performance data.
9. **SEO Engine:** Keyword research, content-gap analysis, and SEO-informed content planning.
10. **Learning Engine:** Applies contextual bandits to continuously refine content decisions.

## Delivery Phases
### Phase 1: MVP (Core Loop)
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn + Instagram.
- **Features:** Brand onboarding, Competitor analysis (baseline), Strategy generation, Content calendar, Content generation, Human approval workflow, Publishing, Baseline analytics.

### Phase 2: Intelligence Expansion
- SEO Engine, Social listening, Campaign management, Content repurposing, Expanded analytics/reporting dashboards, additional platforms (X, YouTube).

### Phase 3: Learning Systems
- Learning Engine (contextual bandits, experimentation), Predictive performance scoring, Self-adjusting planner.

### Phase 4: AI CMO
- Autonomous campaign optimization, Budget optimization, Paid ad creative generation, Cross-channel attribution.

## Critical Notes
- The product is an **AI marketing decision engine**, not just a content generator.
- Human approval is mandatory for all published content in MVP.
